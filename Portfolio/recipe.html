Classic Burger

A delicious homemade burger with melted cheese, fresh vegetables, and special sauce. Perfect for family gatherings or weekend cookouts.

Serves: 5 Cook Time: 30min Difficulty: Normal

  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 4 burger buns
  • 4 slices cheddar cheese
  • 2 tomatoes, sliced
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 4 lettuce leaves
  • 1/4 cup mayonnaise
  • 1/4 cup ketchup

  1. Divide the ground beef into 4 equal portions and shape into patties.
  2. Season both sides of each patty with salt and pepper to taste.
  3. Heat a grill or skillet over medium-high heat.
  4. Cook the patties for 4-5 minutes per side, or until they reach your desired doneness.
  5. During the last minute of cooking, place a slice of cheese on each patty and allow it to melt.
  6. Toast the burger buns lightly on the grill or in a toaster.
  7. Mix mayonnaise and ketchup together to create the sauce.
  8. Assemble burgers: spread sauce on both halves of the bun, add lettuce, tomato, the cooked patty with cheese, and onion slices.
  9. Serve immediately and enjoy!
